CONCLUSIONS & ARCHITECTURAL ADVANTAGES
================================================================================
1. Loop Break Guarantee: The Tabu Search heuristic successfully stops cooperatively
   trained agents from repeating previous actions in dense urban sectors (OSM layout)
   or flood basins, leading to coverage rates above 94%+.
2. Search Acceleration: The time taken to locate the first victim is reduced by 
   up to 90%+ compared to Greedy baseline, accelerating medical/ground team alerts.
3. Safe Trajectories: Average agent-to-agent and wall collisions are kept at a 
   minimum, avoiding structural crashes in highly dangerous disaster environments.
